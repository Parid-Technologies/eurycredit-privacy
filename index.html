Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

EuryCredit is a digital lending platform operated by Parid Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the EuryCredit mobile application.

1 Information We Collect

To provide our lending services, we request access to the following on your device:

📷

Camera

Used solely to photograph your Ghana Card for identity verification. Images are used only for KYC and are not stored beyond what is necessary.

📍

Location

Collected to confirm that our lending services are available in your region. Not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

👥

Contacts

Accessed to help you fill in next-of-kin details quickly. We do not upload, store, or share your contact list.

🔐

Biometrics

Used to secure access to your account. Biometric data is processed entirely on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

🔔

Notifications

Sent for loan status updates, repayment reminders, and account activity. You can disable these at any time in your device settings.

📋

Personal & Financial Data

Name, phone, email, Ghana Card details, employment info, and bank account details — used solely to assess and disburse loans.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and process loan applications
  • To assess creditworthiness and manage your loan account
  • To communicate with you about your account and loans
  • To comply with Ghanaian financial regulations and legal obligations

3 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Identity verification providers (for Ghana Card KYC)
  • Payment processors (for loan disbursement and repayment)
  • Regulatory authorities, where required by Ghanaian law

4 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and as required by applicable financial regulations in Ghana.

6 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

7 Children's Privacy

EuryCredit is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
